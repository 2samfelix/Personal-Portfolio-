Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 use the unfavorable manufacturing costs disclosed in the earnings releases, which Caterpillar said largely reflected higher tariff costs; their bps are derived as the ex-tariff margin computed from raw dollars less Caterpillar's rounded reported margin. Q2 2026 is a different method: Caterpillar disclosed 340 bps on the earnings call (sourced), converted to about $284M here (derived). The two methods are not comparable as a trend. Ex-tariff margin is an **upper bound** on underlying performance: the price realization that lifted it was taken partly to recover the same tariff costs. Separately, Q2 2026 consolidated operating profit included $392m of one-time IEEPA tariff recoveries; the CFO said the majority sits in corporate items. The CI share was not disclosed, so CI segment margin is unlikely to be materially affected — an inference, not a disclosed exclusion.
